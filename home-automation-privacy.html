Home automation

This page describes a private, single-household Home Assistant installation. It connects to the owner's own Google Nest thermostats through Google's Smart Device Management API. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Privacy policy

  • The app accesses only the owner's own Nest devices and Google account.
  • Thermostat data is stored only on the owner's own server at home. It is not sold, shared or sent to any third party.
  • No data is collected from anyone else.
  • To revoke access, remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
